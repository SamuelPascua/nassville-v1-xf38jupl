# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user pinned: "archivos .html, .js, .css"). Libraries from CDN only where a technique needs them (Three.js for the 3D logo, GSAP for scroll motion). No build step for v1.

## Users

*Inferred from the brand's Instagram material, not confirmed:* young streetwear buyers (roughly 16–30) in Spain, mostly Zaragoza and its scene, who follow the brand on Instagram and arrive from a drop post or story wanting to see the new piece, check if their size is still available, and buy before it sells out.

## Product Purpose

The official online store of **nassville**, a Zaragoza streetwear brand. It showcases the brand's world (campaigns, drops, videos) and lets visitors browse products with price and stock status, and later buy directly in the store. v1 is design only: cart and payments come later.

## Positioning

Drop-based independent label with its own narrative per collection ("Losing Interest", "50003", "Toppings", "NASS" zip-up, CD La Almozara kit, Mascu figure), campaign photography shot by the brand itself (parkings, night streets, studio, trips to Egypt / Lapland), ironic copy ("Actuamos primero, pensamos después, nos lamentamos: nunca", "We choose to believe", "born to belive").

## Operating Context

Visitors come from Instagram (@nassville.inc) on phones first. Drops are announced with countdowns and sell out ("SOLD OUT!" is part of the brand's own video language). Pop-ups and events happen in Zaragoza.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v1: sections for hero, campaign imagery/video, product catalogue (price, sold-out state, sizes), about ("conócenos"), social.
- Cart, checkout and payments: explicitly deferred to a later phase.
- Copy: Spanish with English drop names and claims, as the brand writes on Instagram.
- Product names, prices and stock in v1 are **placeholder data** (user approved), kept in an editable data file; must be replaced with real values.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: oval emblem with a slanted "11" + italic "nassville" wordmark, light grey on dark navy (`img/Logo.jpg`). Secondary mark: "n a s s" in capsule outlines.
- Brand colour: the dark navy of the Losing Interest crewneck and the logo ground.
- Streetwear register; the site must feel like the brand's own feed, not a generic shop.

## Evidence on Hand

- `img/`: 103 Instagram exports (campaigns, product flats, posters, packaging).
- `videos/`: 17 Instagram videos (drop films, BTS, teasers).
- No real prices, stock, customer reviews or press: do not fabricate them as facts.
- Social: Instagram @nassville.inc and web nassville.com appear on brand posters; other networks unconfirmed.

## Product Principles

1. The drop is the event: what is new and what is left must be obvious at a glance.
2. Show the brand's own pictures and films; they are the proof.
3. Mobile first, because the audience arrives from Instagram.
4. Say it like the brand says it: short, ironic, bilingual.
